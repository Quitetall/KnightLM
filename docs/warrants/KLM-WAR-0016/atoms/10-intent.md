# Intent

Turn the Assistant board into a match surface. A human can play a model casually or
competitively; two models can play while the user watches. The match loop validates moves,
enforces clocks/retries/failure policy, persists state and exports PGN and logs.

Out of scope: tournament scheduling/ratings (W-06), Lua lessons (R2).
