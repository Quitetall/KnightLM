# Intent

Run model-vs-model tournaments and maintain one trustworthy Glicko-2 ladder. Formats are
Swiss, knockout and double round robin; scheduling is persistent, concurrent within limits
and resumable. Ratings are earned only under the fixed standard-v1 ruleset.

Out of scope: human ratings, assisted/tool ladder, cloud leaderboards.
